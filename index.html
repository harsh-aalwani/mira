Local-First Architecture

Your information deserves a private place.

MIRA is designed with a local-first architecture, allowing processing, indexing, retrieval, and local model inference to happen locally where supported.

This makes it suitable for private documents, internal reports, meeting recordings, screenshots, sensitive organizational information, and offline environments.

Product Story

Making information easier to find, understand, and verify.

MIRA began as a Smart India Hackathon project and has evolved toward a general-purpose multimodal retrieval and assistant platform. The project is under active development.

Built with a focus on multimodal understanding, retrieval quality, usability, and source traceability.
