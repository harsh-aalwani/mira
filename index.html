Core Beliefs

Your knowledge is more valuable than the file it's stored in.

Private documents deserve private workflows.

When you examine confidential strategy, human resource records, or financial projections, your analysis should happen on machines you control—not on servers owned by cloud vendors.

Research shouldn't have to become a cloud dependency.

Field researchers, archivists, and intelligence analysts often work in restricted or offline settings. True assistance must operate without an active internet umbilical cord.

Internal knowledge should remain under your control.

Your organization’s documents are your primary intellectual competitive advantage. Retrieval should empower your team without relinquishing custody of the underlying data.

Offline environments shouldn't mean giving up intelligent retrieval.

You shouldn't be penalized with primitive keyword search just because you prioritize security. Modern hardware is capable of running state-of-the-art multimodal retrieval locally.
